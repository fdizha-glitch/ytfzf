Our Story

The legendary Mukombe Bar is the beating heart of Stream Valley Lodge. Named after the traditional African drinking vessel carved from wood, our bar has been the gathering place for adventurers, storytellers, and dreamers for over two decades.

Under thatched roofs and wicker lanterns, with the African bush as your backdrop, this is where the day's safari tales come alive. Pull up a stool, grab an ice-cold Castle Lager, and become part of the legend.

"The best stories are told with a cold drink in hand and the African sunset as witness."
